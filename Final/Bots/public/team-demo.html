Team Website Simulation

This page demonstrates how your team's website interacts with the Sohrai AI Bot widget. Look at the bottom-right corner for the floating chat bubble.

⚡ Test Conversation History Persistence:

1. Click the floating chat bubble (💬) at the bottom right.

2. Ask the bot any question (e.g. "Best waterfalls in Jharkhand?").

3. Refresh this page (F5 or Ctrl+R) — notice that your conversation and the AI's reply are completely preserved!

Method 1: Drop-In 1-Line Script

Your team can paste this single line right before the closing </body> tag on any HTML page:

<script src="http://localhost:3000/widget.js" defer></script>

Method 2: Inline Iframe

Embed the chat directly into a dedicated webpage or sidebar container:

<iframe src="http://localhost:3000/embed.html" width="100%" height="600"></iframe>

Method 3: Direct API Call

Your team can send JSON requests directly to the backend endpoint:

POST http://localhost:3000/api/chat
{ "message": "...", "history": [...], "lang": "en" }
