<p id="mwCg">The <b id="mwCw">Lodh Falls</b> (also known as <b id="mwDA">Budha Ghagh</b>) is a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterfall" title="Waterfall" id="mwDQ">waterfall</a> on the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burha_River" title="Burha River" id="mwDg">Burha River</a> in the mid-forest  of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palamau_Tiger_Reserve" title="Palamau Tiger Reserve" id="mwDw">Palamau Tiger Reserve</a>, in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latehar_district" title="Latehar district" id="mwEA">Latehar district</a> of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jharkhand" title="Jharkhand" id="mwEQ">Jharkhand</a> state in India. It is the highest waterfall in Jharkhand and the 21st <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_waterfalls_in_India_by_height" title="List of waterfalls in India by height" id="mwEg">highest waterfall in India</a>.<sup about="#mwt14" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-database_1-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"database"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-database-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-database-1" id="mwEw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwFA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFQ">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFg">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt17" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-2" id="mwFw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwGA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGQ">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGg">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt20" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-3"}}'><a href="#cite_note-3" id="mwGw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwHA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHQ">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" id="mwHw" data-mw='{"autoGenerated":true}'/></section><section data-mw-section-id="1" id="mwIA" aria-labelledby="Waterfall"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Waterfall">Waterfall</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Lodh_Falls&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Waterfall"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwIQ">It is located on the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burha_River" title="Burha River" id="mwIg">Burha River</a>, deep in the forest of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latehar_district" title="Latehar district" id="mwIw">Latehar district</a> the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chota_Nagpur_Plateau" title="Chota Nagpur Plateau" id="mwJA">Chota Nagpur Plateau</a>.<sup about="#mwt24" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-lodh_4-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"lodh"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-lodh-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-lodh-4" id="mwJQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwJg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJw">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKA">]</span></span></a></sup> The Lodh Falls is a tiered waterfall with multiple distinct drops in a relatively close succession. It is <span about="#mwt21" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwKQ" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"convert","href":"./Template:Convert"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"143"},"2":{"wt":"m"}},"i":0}}]}'>143 metres (469</span><span typeof="mw:Entity" about="#mwt21"> </span><span about="#mwt21">ft)</span> high.<sup about="#mwt27" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwKg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwKw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwLA">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwLQ">]</span></span></a></sup> The thundering sound of the fall is audible even 10<span typeof="mw:Entity" id="mwLg"> </span>km away.<sup about="#mwt30" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwLw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwMA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwMQ">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwMg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwMw">The Lodh Falls is an example of a nick point caused by rejuvenation. Knick point, also called a nick point or simply nick, represents breaks in slopes in the longitudinal profile of a river caused by rejuvenation. The break in channel gradient allows water to fall vertically giving rise to a waterfall.<sup about="#mwt33" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-7" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-7"}}'><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwNA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwNQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwNg">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwNw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
