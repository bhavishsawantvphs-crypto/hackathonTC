<p id="mwDQ"><b id="mwDg">Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary</b> is a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildlife_sanctuary" title="Wildlife sanctuary" class="mw-redirect" id="mwDw">wildlife sanctuary</a> in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India" title="India" id="mwEA">Indian</a> state of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jharkhand" title="Jharkhand" id="mwEQ">Jharkhand</a>. It was inaugurated in 1975 and provides habitat for an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_elephant" title="Indian elephant" id="mwEg">Indian elephant</a> population.<sup about="#mwt16" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-1" id="mwEw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwFA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFQ">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwWQ">There are thought to be 85 elephants in the sanctuary as of 2024.<sup about="#mwt30" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwWg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwWw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXA">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXQ">]</span></span></a></sup> They migrate to West Bengal during August and September and usually return in December and January.<sup about="#mwt33" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwXg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwXw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwYA">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwYQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
