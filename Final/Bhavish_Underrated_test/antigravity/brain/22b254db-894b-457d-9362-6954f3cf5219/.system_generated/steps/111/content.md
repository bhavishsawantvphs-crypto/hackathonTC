<p id="mwCQ"><b id="mwCg">Netarhat</b> is a hill station in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latehar_district" title="Latehar district" id="mwCw">Latehar district</a><sup about="#mwt15" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-1" id="mwDA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwDQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwDg">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwDw">]</span></span></a></sup> in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India" title="India" id="mwEA">Indian</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_and_territories_of_India" title="States and territories of India" class="mw-redirect" id="mwEQ">state</a> of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jharkhand" title="Jharkhand" id="mwEg">Jharkhand</a>.<sup about="#mwt18" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-gumla_2-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"gumla"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-gumla-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-gumla-2" id="mwEw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwFA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFQ">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFg">]</span></span></a></sup> The hill station is among the highest points of the Chotanagpur plateau. It is known for the natural beauty of hills, forest and waterfalls.<sup about="#mwt21" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-TelegraphIndia2018_3-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"TelegraphIndia2018"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-TelegraphIndia2018-3"}}'><a href="#cite_note-TelegraphIndia2018-3" id="mwFw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwGA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGQ">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGg">]</span></span></a></sup> It is also referred to as the "Queen of Chotanagpur".<sup about="#mwt24" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-bihar_4-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"bihar"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-bihar-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-bihar-4" id="mwGw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwHA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHQ">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHg">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt27" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwHw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwIA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIQ">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
